# 森野 Zoo

Palette: forest #003c33, white #ffffff, stone #eeece7, ink #17171c, leaf #edfce9, editorial coral #ff7759. DESIGN.md supplies the flat surfaces, media radius of 22px, and pill actions.

Type: Space Grotesk / system sans for Latin display; Microsoft JhengHei / PingFang TC for Chinese. Regular weight, tightly spaced display type, generous body line height. Local fallbacks avoid blocking fonts.

Layout: a forest photograph fills a rounded entrance panel; left-aligned oversized headline sits within its quiet dark region. A real red panda photograph creates a smaller discovery window at the right. A white field-guide section uses image-heavy cards; a deep-green landscape section introduces habitats; a restrained conservation band and ordered route finish the journey.

Mobile: headline and image share one forest entrance, then single-column photo cards; bottom navigation and a compact persistent red panda guide remain reachable. Modal details become a bottom sheet.

Review: the forest and a photographic animal encounter carry the identity. Avoid dashboard metrics and repeated reveal effects. Continuous motion is limited to the companion's slow breathing and a few forest motes; all stop when hidden or reduced motion is requested. Animal entry animates deliberately on user action. Photographs are locally optimized and lazy-loaded after the hero.
